How it works

From your finger to your doctor’s decision

01

The ring measures

Blood pressure, heart rate, SpO₂, temperature, sleep and steps — automatically and discreetly.

02

The app syncs

The app filters out invalid readings and sends your data securely. You follow your own progress.

03

Your team is alerted

Limits set just for you trigger real-time alerts on your care team’s dashboard.

04

Your doctor acts

A message, a new reading, a video visit, prescriptions and lab orders. Everything goes into your record.

Real-time sync requires your phone to be connected to the ring.

Security and privacy

Your data belongs to you. Protected at every step.

Linked access

Each clinician sees only their own patients, and only with your active consent.

Audit trail

Every view and change to your record is logged with author and time.

Locked records

Signed notes can’t be edited. Corrections are added as addenda.

Discreet notifications

Phone alerts never show clinical values on the lock screen.

Private files

Documents in private storage, opened through temporary links.

Recorded consent

Terms, privacy and sharing are versioned and dated — and you can revoke them.

Built around HIPAA (US) and LGPD (Brazil) requirements.
